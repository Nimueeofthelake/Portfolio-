Hello!

Hello! My name is Alina. I am a freshman majoring in AI & Machine Learning. I am taking this course because AI is a new technology, and it makes me excited to see what it can be turned into in the future. I eventually want to become a researcher and help develop AI into a more productive tool. From this course, I hope to get a deeper understanding of AI and how it can be expanded upon.

In some instances, AI has become a tool. In my daily life, I often have written portions for my assignments. I use the Gemini feature on Google Docs to correct my spelling and grammar. Something concerning I find about AI is the environmental impact it has on the environment. The course objective I am most interested in studying is the ethics of AI. This personally matters to me because I believe technology should not just be useful but also ethical so that it can be the most productive tool. Outside of class, I often enjoy playing my violin and doodling. I have been playing the violin since 4th grade and have been in orchestra for just as long. Now I just play for fun.
