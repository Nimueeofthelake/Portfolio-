What I hope to get out of this course is a deeper understanding of how artificial intelligence works. I am interested in learning more about the technology behind AI and how it continues to improve and affect our everyday lives.

AI in My Life

AI has already shown up in my life in different ways. For example, I have used AI for grammar and writing help on essays in high school, including tools like ChatGPT.

A Concern About AI

One thing that concerns me about AI is its environmental impact, especially its effect on water resources. As AI technology continues to grow, I think it is important to consider not only what AI can do, but also the environmental resources required to develop and use it.

Ethics and AI

Out of all the course objectives, I am most interested in identifying and discussing ethical issues surrounding AI. This matters to me because I believe the impact of a technology is just as important as how we use it. AI has so much potential for improvement and innovation, but it is also important to think about the consequences that can come with its use.
